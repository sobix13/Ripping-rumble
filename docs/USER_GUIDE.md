# User Guide

Press **Join** while a lobby is open. Leaving is available before the lobby closes. After the start, the bot runs every scene, elimination, return and final. No extra player click, wallet connection, signature or purchase is needed.

The scenes draw on Hot Wheels history and collector culture across the wider hobby. A named model does not claim RipCars inventory, ownership or a real item award. This is an independent community game.

## Follow the game

- **Out** lists members eliminated in that lap.
- **Back in** lists members returning to the game.
- **Still in** shows the remaining field once it is small.
- **Holding** shows fictional story items. Transfers move the same item. Elimination drops the current item.
- Automatic **Second Chance** chooses one eligible fallen member when its window opens. Each member returns at most once. Some games have no suitable window.

Members do not choose their actions. Luck and the automated rules decide the outcome. Fictional item recall does not imply ownership of a real Hot Wheels or a guaranteed advantage.

## Results and help

Use `/rumble leaderboard`, `/rumble stats` and `/rumble worlds`. Open `/rumble guide` for the same member guidance inside Discord. The final result includes the full ranking, inline or as an attached file. Simultaneous victims share a place.

If a game stops, contact a host. An interrupted game does not resume from its previous lap. A finished result remains in storage while a failed announcement is reviewed or retried.
